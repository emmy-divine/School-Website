Admissions

Join the Divine Destiny Family

Everything you need to apply — requirements, process, fees, FAQs, and the full online admission form.

Admission Process

Four simple steps to enrolment.

1

Submit Application

Complete the online admission form below with student and parent details.

2

Document Review

Our admissions office reviews your documents within 3 working days.

3

Assessment & Interview

Shortlisted candidates attend a short placement assessment and interview.

4

Offer & Enrolment

Successful candidates receive an offer letter and complete registration.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed online admission form
  • Passport photograph (recent)
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Previous school result / report card
  • Transfer certificate (for transferring students)
  • Immunisation record (Nursery & Primary)

School Fees Enquiry

Fees vary by class and school (Academy or Comprehensive). For the current session's fee schedule and payment plans, request a call-back or chat with our admissions office directly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the admission session open? ⌄
Applications for the 2026/2027 session are open now. We admit into Nursery through SSS1 for the new session, with limited mid-session transfers considered on a case-by-case basis.
Is there an entrance assessment? ⌄
Yes. Shortlisted candidates from Primary 1 upward sit a short placement assessment in Literacy and Numeracy, followed by a brief interview with a parent or guardian present.
Can I pay school fees in instalments? ⌄
Termly payment plans are available on request. Please reach the admissions office via WhatsApp or the contact form to discuss a plan that works for your family.
Do you accept mid-term transfers? ⌄
Yes, subject to space availability in the requested class and a review of the student's previous academic records.
What documents do I need at registration? ⌄
Birth certificate, passport photographs, previous school result, and (if transferring) a transfer certificate. Immunisation records are required for Nursery and Primary applicants.
