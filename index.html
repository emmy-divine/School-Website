A Word From The Principal

"We do not just teach children to pass examinations — we raise them to know who they are, and Whose they are."

Every child who walks through our gates is welcomed into a family that takes their spiritual formation as seriously as their academic results. Our teachers are mentors first, instructors second — and it shows in the discipline, confidence, and character our students carry long after they graduate.

Mr. Umar Emmanuel
Principal, Divine Destiny Schools
Who We Are

Two schools, one destiny — since 2016.

Founded by Mr. Emmanuel Umar, Divine Destiny Schools began as a single Nursery and Primary school with a simple conviction: that quality, faith-based education should be within every family's reach.

Today, that conviction has grown into Divine Destiny Academy (Nursery & Primary) and Divine Destiny Comprehensive Academy (Junior & Senior Secondary) — two schools, unified in vision, preparing learners for WAEC, NECO and JAMB while shaping character that outlasts any certificate.

Mr. Emmanuel Umar Founder, Divine Destiny Schools · Est. 2016
