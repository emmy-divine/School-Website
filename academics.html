Academics

Curriculum Built for Every Stage

From Nursery play-based learning to SSS3 WAEC preparation — one connected academic journey.

Curriculum

A connected journey, Nursery to SSS3.

We follow the Nigerian Curriculum, preparing students for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB, delivered across three stages under our two schools.

Divine Destiny Academy

Nursery

Ages 2 – 5
  • Phonics & early literacy
  • Number work & sorting
  • Creative & motor play
  • Bible stories & character
Divine Destiny Academy

Primary

Primary 1 – 6
  • English Studies & Literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Basic Science & Technology
  • Christian Religious Studies
  • Verbal & Quantitative Reasoning
Divine Destiny Comprehensive

Secondary

JSS1 – SSS3
  • Core & elective subjects (Sciences, Arts, Commercial)
  • WAEC & NECO examination preparation
  • JAMB / UTME coaching
  • Trade & vocational electives
Subjects Offered

Core & elective subjects by stage.

Nursery & Primary

English Studies, Mathematics, Basic Science & Technology, Christian Religious Studies, Social Studies, Verbal & Quantitative Reasoning, Handwriting, Phonics, Creative Arts, Physical & Health Education, Computer Studies.

Junior Secondary (JSS1–JSS3)

English Language, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, Business Studies, Christian Religious Studies, Social Studies, Civic Education, French, Computer Studies, Agricultural Science, Creative & Cultural Arts.

Senior Secondary — Sciences

English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Mathematics, Agricultural Science, Geography, Computer Studies.

Senior Secondary — Arts & Commercial

English Language, Mathematics, Literature-in-English, Government, Economics, Commerce, Accounting, CRS, History, Civic Education.

Learning Approach

Child-centred, activity-based learning that builds understanding before speed — with faith and character woven into every subject.

Teaching Methodology

Small class sizes, continuous assessment-driven instruction, practical demonstrations, and dedicated remediation for struggling learners.

Assessment

Continuous assessment (tests, assignments, projects) plus end-of-term examinations, reported each term via the Parent Portal.

Academic Calendar

3 terms, one focused academic year.

The academic session runs across three terms (September–July), with mid-term breaks, examination weeks, and holiday assignments published each term. Download the full calendar and curriculum outline below.

Download Academic Calendar Download Curriculum

2026/2027 Session at a Glance

  • First Term: September – December
  • Second Term: January – April
  • Third Term: April – July (WAEC/NECO/JAMB for SSS3)
  • Mid-term breaks: Week 6 of each term
