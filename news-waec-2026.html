Congratulations to Our 2026 WAEC Graduating Class

June 30, 2026

We are proud to celebrate the SSS3 graduating class of 2026, who once again sustained Divine Destiny Comprehensive Academy's strong record in the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WAEC).

Consistent with recent sessions, roughly 80% of our candidates obtained credit passes in five subjects or more, including English Language and Mathematics — comfortably above the national average.

This result reflects the dedication of our teaching staff, the discipline instilled from Nursery through Secondary, and the consistent support of parents throughout each student's academic journey.

Graduating students are now being guided through JAMB/UTME preparation and university placement support as they move into the next stage of their education.
