About Divine Destiny Schools

Faith, Discipline & Excellence Since 2016

The story, vision, values and people behind Divine Destiny Academy and Divine Destiny Comprehensive Academy.

Our History

From one classroom to two schools.

Founded in 2016 by Mr. Emmanuel Umar, Divine Destiny Schools was established with the vision of providing quality, affordable, and faith-based education that develops students spiritually, academically, morally, socially, and emotionally.

The institution began as Divine Destiny Academy and has grown into a respected educational community serving learners from Nursery through Senior Secondary School. Following Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) approval, the secondary school now operates officially as Divine Destiny Comprehensive Academy, while the Nursery and Primary sections continue under the name Divine Destiny Academy.

Today, both schools operate under the unified identity of Divine Destiny Schools, preparing learners for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB while nurturing leadership, discipline, integrity, and excellence.

Our Vision

To raise well-rounded children who are spiritually grounded, academically excellent, and morally upright, empowering them to make a positive impact on their generation and beyond.

Our Mission

To provide quality and affordable education through dedicated, highly qualified teachers in a nurturing environment that promotes academic excellence, moral integrity, spiritual growth, and lifelong learning.

What We Stand On

Our Core Values

Godliness

Faith at the center of everything we do.

Discipline

Order and structure that shapes character.

Excellence

High standards, inside and outside class.

Integrity

Honesty that holds, seen or unseen.

Service

Raising children who give back generously.

Founder

Mr. Emmanuel Umar

Mr. Emmanuel Umar founded Divine Destiny Schools in 2016 with a conviction that quality, faith-based education should be within reach of every family, not just the privileged few. That conviction remains the foundation on which both Divine Destiny Academy and Divine Destiny Comprehensive Academy are built.

Mr. Emmanuel Umar Founder & Proprietor, Divine Destiny Schools
